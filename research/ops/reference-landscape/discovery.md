## DISC-001. Object-centric event logs as a research exchange, not as a kernel

- Evidence: EV-001, EV-002
- Why it is not a restatement of #5, #38, or #73

#5 asks what an Event is. #38 mines EPCIS visibility events. #73 parks CMMN discretionary cases. None of those folders opened a log in which one occurrence binds many typed objects with role qualifiers, standing object-to-object links, and timed attribute changes, and in which a case id is refused.

The discovery is not "add OCEL to RFC-0001." The discovery is a falsification tool. If OS Event plus Link cannot encode an OCEL 2.0 log from a real order-to-cash or procure-to-pay extract without inventing a case, LAW-001 gains weight. If it can, LAW-001 dies and OCEL remains a scenario format.

A later corpus unit can ingest SRC-OCEL-20 and the 2.1 format pages. Sample public logs on ocel-standard.org include SAP ERP IDES P2P and O2C extracts. Those logs are evidence of flattening pressure. They are not SAP product semantics.

## DISC-002. BFO-based industrial ontology as a second formal adversary

- Evidence: EV-007
- Why it is not a restatement of #37 or #38

#37 opened UFO and OntoUML. #38 opened ISA-95 and ISA-88. IOF Core is a Released mid-level industrial ontology under BFO 2020. The 2026-05-28 release adds Allen-like constraints between plan specifications and recipe-occurrence classifiers.

UFO and BFO disagree about several foundational cuts. Using only UFO as the formal adversary hides that fight. The child, if filed, should ask whether plan-specification temporal constraints and recipe-occurrence classification are already covered by #14 and ISA-88, or whether BFO's occurrent and continuant split pressures RFC-0001 differently from UFO's endurant and perdurant split.

Do not import IOF OWL. Do not open a "manufacturing ontology" module issue.
