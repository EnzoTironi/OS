## Evidence kinds

Every finding is tagged as one of these kinds.

- **Domain evidence.** A real-world distinction forced by operations, law, metrology, or accounting practice.
- **Source-system artifact.** A product mechanism that may not belong in OS.
- **Candidate law.** The smallest claim that would explain the evidence. Never silently accepted.
- **Counterexample.** A case that would break a candidate law.
- **Runtime consequence.** What a surviving claim would force an engine to keep observable.

## Decision vocabulary

Use only `hypothesis`, `supported`, `rejected`, or `undetermined`.
